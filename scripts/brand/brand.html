chdman, improved

Our own fork of MAME 0.289’s chdman: faster, bugs fixed. Same CHD files and checksums as the desktop tool.

Knows your games

Reads the disc for its console, serial, disc number and region, and names it from 42,000 Redump entries.

Multi-core fast

Compression runs on every CPU core, with SIMD when your browser supports it.

Made for phones

Fits any screen, notch and text size. Save to Files on iPhone, dark mode included.

Big discs welcome

Results stream to disk, not memory, so multi-gigabyte DVD images work too.

Totally private

Nothing leaves your device, and it works offline: downloaded, or installed from the web.

The right CHD

CD or DVD, and the hunk size each console’s emulators want, set when the game is recognized.

Boots as it should

Saturn and Sega CD CHDs skip the cue sheet their emulators reject; NAOMI GD-ROMs keep their romset name.

Warns in time

Says when an emulator can’t read Zstd, or misreads a disc’s sessions, gaps or tracks, before you convert.

LibCrypt .sbi

Saved next to a PAL PlayStation CHD, made from a CloneCD .sub, and asked for when a protected game has none.

Whole sets

An .m3u playlist for games on several discs, and a note of the discs a set still needs.

MiSTer preset

Zstd and FLAC in small hunks, fast enough for MiSTer’s CD cores, and a warning where it has none.

Android PS2

CD CHDs of DVD games, the only kind AetherSX2 and NetherSX2 read.

Says what won’t

GameCube, Wii, Xbox, PS3 and video discs are recognized, with a note that their players skip CHDs.

Refuses what breaks

Discs chdman 0.289 would store wrong, such as CD+G tracks, get a card that says why instead of a bad CHD.
